Questions

Do I need to be technical?

No. You use it through apps and a wall dashboard. We handle the setup, and Care covers updates and fixes.

Is it secure?

We open no ports to the internet, use an encrypted private network for remote access, keep backups, and update in planned windows. No system is unhackable. We keep yours simple, current and backed up.

Where do you install?

No travel fee across the Phoenix metro. Elsewhere in Arizona it’s $150 under a 2-hour drive and $300 beyond. Out of state we charge travel at cost plus $500 per travel day, once we’ve confirmed that state’s licensing.

Who does the wiring?

A licensed low-voltage partner handles any wiring and mounting and bills you for that part directly.

What if I change my mind?

Your deposit is 100% refundable until parts are ordered. After that, the setup portion is refunded and parts go back under each store’s return policy, minus any restocking fees.

Can I just buy the parts myself?

Yes. The builder is free and shows the full parts list with store links. Bring us in later if you get stuck.

Will it save me money on subscriptions?

Usually not overall. It can replace some cloud storage and app subscriptions, but the hardware and setup cost more. Buy it for ownership and privacy, not savings.
