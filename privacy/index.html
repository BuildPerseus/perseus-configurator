Privacy

The short version: we collect only what you type into our forms, we use it to talk with you about Perseus Lab, and we don’t sell it.

Last updated: November 25, 2026

What we collect

Only what you enter in a form on this site: your name, email, and anything else you choose to add, such as your business name, phone number, team size, city or notes. If you book a call through our calendar, you also enter your name, email and a time. The builder at /build runs in your browser; your answers there aren’t sent anywhere unless you submit a quote request.

How we use it

  • To reply to you, set up a call, and send a quote or proposal.
  • To contact you when something you joined a waitlist for opens.

We don’t sell or rent your information, and we don’t add you to marketing lists you didn’t ask for.

Who else sees it

  • Cloudflare hosts this site. Like most hosts, it keeps basic technical logs such as IP addresses.
  • Formspree receives what you submit in our forms and emails it to us. It keeps a copy for a limited time under its own privacy policy.
  • Cal.com runs our booking calendar. If you pick a call time there, Cal.com receives the name, email and any notes you enter, under its own privacy policy.
  • Our email provider, so messages can reach us.
  • This site loads its fonts from Google Fonts, which means your browser contacts Google when a page loads.

We don’t use advertising trackers or analytics on this site today. If that changes, we’ll update this page first.

If you become a client

Client work is covered by your written agreement. You own your AI accounts; we tell you in writing which services see your data, and we remove our access whenever you ask.

Links to stores

Some pages link to stores that sell parts. Those stores have their own privacy policies. If any link becomes an affiliate link, we’ll say so right next to it.

How long we keep it

We keep form submissions while we’re talking with you or working together, and delete them when you ask, unless we have to keep a record for tax or legal reasons.

Your choices

Ask us at any time to see, correct or delete what you’ve sent us, or to stop contacting you. Reply to any email from us to reach a person.

Changes

If we change this page, we’ll update the date at the top.
