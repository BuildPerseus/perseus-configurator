How done-for-you pricing works

  • Parts plus a 15% sourcing fee, quoted plus sales tax.
  • Setup from $1,500, or from $3,500 with a large AI box.
  • Deposit: the parts plus half the setup. The balance is due at handover.
  • Your deposit is 100% refundable until parts are ordered. After that, setup is refunded and parts go back under each store’s return policy, minus any restocking fees.
  • No travel fee across the Phoenix metro. Elsewhere in Arizona it’s $150 under a 2-hour drive and $300 beyond. Out of state is travel at cost plus $500 per travel day.
  • Quotes are good for 7 days, because parts prices move.
  • Your first 3 months of Care are included. After that, Care starts at $150 a month and is optional.
