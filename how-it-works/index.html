How it works

From a two-minute build to a home that just works, and who to call when it doesn’t.

  1. Build it

    Pick what you want it to do in the builder. You get a recommended build, a parts list and a price range.

  2. 20-minute call

    We confirm what you need, cut anything you don’t, and send a firm quote. Whole-home work gets a site visit first.

  3. Deposit

    Parts plus half the setup. We order parts only after it clears, and it’s fully refundable until we do.

  4. Install day

    We set it up with a licensed partner, test a backup restore with you and walk you through a written guide.

  5. Care

    Your first 3 months are included. After that, Care plans start at $150 a month, or you run it yourself.

How we install

The same standards on every job, so your setup is quiet, safe and easy to look after.

  • Quiet mini PCs, never loud rack gear
  • Mirrored NAS drives and 3-2-1 backups, with a restore tested at handover
  • Battery backup that shuts the server down safely in an outage
  • No ports opened to the internet. Remote access runs over an encrypted private network
  • Your internet keeps working even if the server is off
  • Updates in planned maintenance windows, tested first on our own setup
  • Wiring and mounting by a licensed partner
  • A written handover guide for every install

Questions

Do I need to be technical?

No. You use it through apps and a wall dashboard. We handle the setup, and Care covers updates and fixes.

Is it secure?

We open no ports to the internet, use an encrypted private network for remote access, keep backups, and update in planned windows. No system is unhackable. We keep yours simple, current and backed up.

Where do you install?

No travel fee across the Phoenix metro. Elsewhere in Arizona it’s $150 under a 2-hour drive and $300 beyond. Out of state we charge travel at cost plus $500 per travel day, once we’ve confirmed that state’s licensing.

Who does the wiring?

A licensed low-voltage partner handles any wiring and mounting and bills you for that part directly.

What if I change my mind?

Your deposit is 100% refundable until parts are ordered. After that, the setup portion is refunded and parts go back under each store’s return policy, minus any restocking fees.

Can I just buy the parts myself?

Yes. The builder is free and shows the full parts list with store links. Bring us in later if you get stuck.

Will it save me money on subscriptions?

Usually not overall. It can replace some cloud storage and app subscriptions, but the hardware and setup cost more. Buy it for ownership and privacy, not savings.
